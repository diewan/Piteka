# Runbook — incident response and support

The pilot agreement must name a primary support owner, backup, security contact,
product owner, and customer contact with tested channels and coverage hours.
Role titles in the readiness manifest are placeholders and block launch.

For a suspected unauthorized action, tenant leak, credential exposure,
signature/integrity failure, or unexplained provider result: acknowledge and
open an incident record; stop new dispatch without mutating existing state;
preserve database, evidence objects, logs, environment snapshot, contract pin,
and provider identifiers; rotate or revoke affected credentials through the
approved owner; and reconcile through evidence. Do not infer non-occurrence
from a missing GitHub response.

Communications distinguish facts observed, claims received, verifier
conclusions, and unknowns. Only the incident commander declares containment;
only the release owner resumes dispatch after security and product approval.
Retractions and corrections are append-only. Deletion requests pause when a
documented legal hold applies and otherwise follow signed retention terms.

Close with a timeline, impact and disclosure assessment, evidence/artifact
digests, corrective owners and dates, credential-rotation proof, and a tested
resume decision. Critical or High audit findings keep the pilot stopped.
